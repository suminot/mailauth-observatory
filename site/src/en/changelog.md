# Change log

Changes to how the measurement is made, or to how it is aggregated,
are recorded here. When a figure moves against the previous month,
**this is what tells you whether the world changed or the measurement did.**

This list is generated by `mailauth changelog` (2026-11-04T14:26:24+00:00).
Version changes are detected by machine, but **what they did to the
figures has to be written by a person.** A month whose “interpretation” line is blank is one nobody has written up yet.

compared directly with the previous month's. Past aggregations are not
rebuilt retroactively; they are kept as history.
