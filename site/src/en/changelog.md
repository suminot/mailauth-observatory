# Change log

Changes to how the measurement is made, or to how it is aggregated,
are recorded here. When a figure moves against the previous month,
**this is what tells you whether the world changed or the measurement did.**

This list is generated by `mailauth changelog` (2026-11-04T19:25:18+00:00).
Version changes are detected by machine, but **what they did to the
figures has to be written by a person.** A month whose “interpretation” line is blank is one nobody has written up yet.

## 2026-11

### Changes to the method this month (written by hand)

- The outbound front-end check now includes the names that Proofpoint, Mimecast, Barracuda, Cisco, Trend Micro and others ask customers to publish in SPF for sending (only names found in each vendor's documentation). The number of US companies with an outbound front-end rose largely because of this.
- Rules were added for DKIM CNAME targets (SendGrid, HubSpot, Constant Contact, Salesforce and others) and SPF includes (KnowBe4, Mailgun, Oracle and Japanese delivery services). The rise in sending-service counts comes from this.
- Inferences without a layer were split into "services used for sending" and "DMARC report aggregation services". Domain-ownership TXT records alone are no longer counted as evidence of sending.
- Whether DMARC report destinations publish the record accepting the reports is now counted.
- MX hosts confirmed in operators' documentation (iCloud+, Seznam, Daum and others) were added to the platform check.
- The SPF terms that make DNS lookups are now counted through the include and redirect targets. Domains over the limit of 10, and domains whose targets have no SPF, were added to the summary.
- Counts by the final SPF `all` term (-all, ~all, ?all, +all, none) were added to the summary.

No change on the measurement side.

## How this is recorded

compared directly with the previous month's. Past aggregations are not
rebuilt retroactively; they are kept as history.
